# Sexual Health Tariff Grouper

The Sexual Health Tariff Grouper is a web application that automates tariff charging for sexual health services. Providers submit their monthly activity data; the Grouper applies the commissioner's agreed tariff configuration — currencies, cross-charging rules and market forces factors — and produces accurate, transparent charges that both providers and commissioners can see.
