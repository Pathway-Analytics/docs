# Case studies

<p class="cs-standfirst">Pathway Analytics delivers healthcare economics consultancy across the NHS, supporting commissioners and providers with data-driven approaches to tariff implementation, pathway costing, and service transformation. The case studies below illustrate how our work has delivered measurable improvements for clients.</p>

<h2 class="pa-index-title"><a href="/case-studies/sexual-health-tariff">Redesigning the funding of sexual health services in England</a></h2>
<p class="pa-index-text">How Pathway Analytics redesigned the funding of sexual health services, from clinical pathways and financial impact modelling to a tariff and digital service still in use across England.</p>
<p class="pa-index-finding"><b>130</b> services in England reported as commissioned solely on the tariff in 2025/26</p>

<h2 class="pa-index-title"><a href="/case-studies/sexual-assault-services">Who should pay for sexual assault services?</a></h2>
<p class="pa-index-text">How Pathway Analytics agreed good-practice pathways for victims of sexual assault, built an economic model of their cost, and proposed how health, policing, justice and local government should share it.</p>
<p class="pa-index-finding"><b>70%+</b> of the modelled cost of good-practice care is advocacy and talking therapies</p>
</li>
