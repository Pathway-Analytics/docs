# Health Pathways

Health Pathways is how Pathway Analytics maps and redesigns care pathways. It shows how people move through services, where they fall away between organisations and what each step costs. That gives commissioners, providers and funders a shared picture to work from when they redesign services, decide where resources go and set funding.
